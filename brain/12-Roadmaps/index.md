# Roadmaps

## Purpose

Evolution timeline, phase plans, and milestones for the Perionyx platform. Tracks every engineering phase from initial architecture through enterprise deployment, including scope, decisions, and outcomes.

**Authority:** Medium — plans reflect intent at time of writing; execution reality may diverge.

## Summary

The Roadmaps folder contains the platform's chronological evolution — 24 phases spanning architecture, security, financial systems, workflow validation, design governance, and public platform architecture. The Evolution Timeline is the single source of truth for what was built, when, and why.
