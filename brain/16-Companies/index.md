# Companies

## Purpose

Target customers, partners, and vendor profiles. Tracks company analysis, opportunity pipelines, and relationship context across the business ecosystem.

**Authority:** Low — company data is time-sensitive and should be validated against current CRM records and market data.

## Summary

The Companies folder maintains structured profiles for all organizations Perionyx engages with — prospects, customers, technology partners, service vendors, and integration partners. Each profile captures company context, opportunity status, relationship mapping, and interaction history.

### Profile Structure

Each company profile should capture:
- **Identity:** Company name, industry, size, geography
- **Context:** Technology stack, current tools, pain points
- **Relationship:** Contact persons, interaction history, deal stage
- **Analysis:** Fit assessment, competitive landscape, timeline
- **Opportunity:** Value estimate, probability, next actions
