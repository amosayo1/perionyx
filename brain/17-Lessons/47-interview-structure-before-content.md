# Interview structure before content

**Category**: Customer Intelligence

**Lesson**: Build the knowledge graph structure before importing interview evidence. When the People profiles, Interview records, Pain Point pages, Validated Evidence pages, and cross-links exist before the first transcript arrives, every import becomes a simple append operation rather than a restructuring exercise. Content without structure is noise; structure without content is a promise.

**When it applies**: When you have customer interview data that needs to be institutionalized in a knowledge base, and the knowledge base does not yet have the pages, templates, or link architecture to receive it.
