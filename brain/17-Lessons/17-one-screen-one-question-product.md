# One Screen, One Question (Product Edition)

**Category**: Product

**Lesson**: This is the product application of lesson #5. Every screen should answer exactly one question: "What is my cash position?" (Cash Dashboard), "What approvals need my attention?" (Approval Queue), "What's the forecast?" (Forecast View). Screens that try to answer multiple questions fragment attention and reduce decision quality. Split aggressively — it's better to have 10 focused screens than 3 overloaded ones.

**When it applies**: When scoping any new page or feature. Write the "one question" on a sticky note and tape it to the requirements. If the screen can't answer it in under 3 seconds, redesign.
