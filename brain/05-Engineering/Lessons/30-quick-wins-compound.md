# Quick Wins Compound

Small, focused improvements compound into dramatic perceived quality gains. A timestamp on a KPI card, a previous-period delta, a confidence badge — each takes minutes to implement but collectively transform a dashboard from "looks like a prototype" to "feels like a product."

## Context

Phase 20.1 fixed 11 workflow friction issues in a single session. The three fastest fixes (Cmd+K wiring, KPI timestamps, previous-period deltas) each took under 30 minutes but collectively addressed 3 of the 12 issues. The ConfidenceBadge component took ~20 minutes but standardized confidence display across 15+ components.

## Principle

Before building large features, audit for quick wins. A 30-minute fix that improves 100 screens is worth more than a 3-day feature that improves 1 screen.

## Application

- Timestamps on financial figures → trust signal (30 min)
- Previous-period deltas on KPIs → change visibility (30 min)
- Deprecation banners on legacy routes → safe transition (20 min)
- Data freshness indicator → staleness awareness (20 min)
- Standardized confidence badge → consistency (20 min)
- Global undo provider → safety net (45 min)

## Anti-Pattern

Don't skip quick wins because they're "too small." Don't defer them to "later" — later never comes. The cumulative impact of 6 quick wins exceeds the impact of 1 large feature.
