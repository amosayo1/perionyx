# CFOs Don't Wait

**Category**: Product

**Lesson**: Metric values render first, charts second. If a CFO has to wait for a chart to see their cash position, the design has failed. The performance hierarchy for financial dashboards is: (1) key metric number, (2) trend indicator, (3) sparkline, (4) full chart, (5) detailed table. Every millisecond of delay on step 1 costs trust. This is why the Executive KPI cards render synchronously while charts lazy-load.

**When it applies**: When designing any dashboard or data visualization for financial users. The number comes first. Always. Charts and tables are supporting evidence, not the primary answer.
