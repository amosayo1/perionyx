# Customer Intelligence

## Purpose

This folder is the canonical knowledge base for all customer-facing intelligence in Perionyx. Every interview, pain point, validated evidence claim, product hypothesis, and design partner relationship lives here. It is the single source of truth for "what do our customers and prospects actually need?"

## Summary

Customer Intelligence is built from direct conversations with finance professionals across MENA and global markets. Evidence flows from interviews → validated evidence → product principles → product decisions. The CRM module (`src/modules/crm/`) stores operational contact data; this folder stores the knowledge derived from those relationships.
