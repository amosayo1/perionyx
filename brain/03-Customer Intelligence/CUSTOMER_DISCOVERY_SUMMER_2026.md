## Lessons Learned

1. **Structure before content** — Creating the Brain CI structure first made imports systematic (Lesson 47)
2. **CRM ≠ Brain** — CRM stores operational data; Brain stores knowledge. Both are needed.
3. **Partial evidence is still evidence** — Ahmed Shatla's referenced quote is valuable even without full transcript
4. **Knowledge compounds** — When every conversation becomes structured evidence, patterns emerge (Lesson 54)
5. **Evidence traceability matters** — Linking people → evidence → principles → decisions makes product decisions defensible
