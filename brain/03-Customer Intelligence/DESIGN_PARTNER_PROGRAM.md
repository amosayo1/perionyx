# Design Partner Program

## Purpose

Tracks design partner candidates, their potential contributions, and scoring. Design partners are finance professionals who actively help shape the product through feedback, validation, and testing.

## Summary

Design partners are scored on 5 dimensions: Engagement Depth, Domain Expertise, Feedback Quality, Strategic Value, and Availability. Scores range from 1-10 per dimension (max 50). Only contacts with 2+ completed interviews are eligible for full scoring.

## Scoring Framework

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Engagement Depth** | 10 | How deeply engaged is this person? (1 = LinkedIn connection only, 10 = actively co-building) |
| **Domain Expertise** | 10 | How deep is their finance/ERP knowledge? (1 = general awareness, 10 = 15+ years domain expert) |
| **Feedback Quality** | 10 | How actionable is their feedback? (1 = vague, 10 = specific workflows + pain points + suggestions) |
| **Strategic Value** | 10 | How valuable is their network/position? (1 = individual contributor, 10 = CFO/VP with 100+ person team) |
| **Availability** | 10 | How available are they for ongoing collaboration? (1 = one-time only, 10 = weekly check-ins) |

### Score Tiers

| Tier | Score Range | Commitment Level |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Tier 1 — Co-Builder** | 40-50 | Weekly calls, early access, direct Slack channel |
| **Tier 2 — Design Partner** | 30-39 | Bi-weekly calls, beta access, dedicated feedback channel |
| **Tier 3 — Advisory Board** | 20-29 | Monthly calls, feature previews, survey participation |
| **Tier 4 — Community** | 10-19 | Quarterly updates, public roadmap, community forum |
