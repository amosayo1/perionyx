# People

## Purpose

Team members, advisors, and contact records. Maps expertise, roles, and relationships across the Perionyx ecosystem.

**Authority:** Low — personal information should be verified and updated regularly; privacy obligations apply.

## Summary

The People folder maintains profiles for team members, advisors, and key contacts. Each profile captures role, expertise areas, project involvement, and relationship context. Supports both internal team coordination and external stakeholder management.

### Profile Structure

Each person profile should capture:
- **Identity:** Name, role, organization, contact
- **Expertise:** Domain areas, technical skills, certifications
- **Involvement:** Projects, phases, decisions contributed to
- **Relationships:** Connections to other people, companies, customers
- **History:** Interactions, meetings, notes
