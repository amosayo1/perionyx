# Market Intelligence

## Purpose

Competitor profiles, market trends, industry analysis, and competitive positioning for the Enterprise Financial Operating System space. Informs product strategy and go-to-market decisions.

**Authority:** Low — market data is time-sensitive and should be validated against current sources before making strategic decisions.

## Summary

The Market Intelligence folder captures the external landscape: who we compete with, where the market is heading, and how Perionyx positions against alternatives. Includes competitive analysis, feature comparisons, pricing intelligence, and industry trend tracking.
