# EDP 23.1 — Constitutional Validation

**Phase**: 23.1 — Constitutional Validation
**Date**: 2026-07-24
**Decision**: Validate the Platform Constitution against actual codebase implementation
**Status**: Accepted

---

## Context

Phase 23.0 created the Platform Constitution (32 documents, 15 Architectural Laws, 15 Platforms). This validation verifies that the Constitution reflects reality, not aspiration. Without validation, the Constitution is a design document, not an engineering authority.

---

## Decision

Conduct a comprehensive, evidence-based validation of all 15 Laws, 15 Platforms, the AP reference implementation, canonical model, dependencies, security, observability, and governance.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Skip Validation
- **Pros**: Save time
- **Constitution loses authority** — becomes aspirational, not enforced
- **Verdict**: Rejected

### Alternative 2: Validate Only Laws (Not Platforms)
- **Pros**: Faster
- Misses maturity gaps that affect Laws 3, 8, 9, 10
- **Verdict**: Rejected — too narrow

### Alternative 3: Full Validation (Chosen)
- **Pros**: Complete picture. Evidence-based. Actionable.
- **Con**: More effort (3 parallel audit agents)
- **Verdict**: Accepted
