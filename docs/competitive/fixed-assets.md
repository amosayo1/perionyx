## Key Advantages

### 1. AI-Powered Asset Intelligence
Four recommendation types (replacement, maintenance, impairment, utilization) plus depreciation anomaly detection — no competitor offers AI-native fixed asset capabilities.

### 2. Unified Maintenance & Asset Management
Integrated maintenance tracking with kanban board. SAP requires separate PM module. Oracle/Dynamics/Workday/NetSuite lack integrated maintenance.

### 3. Comprehensive Impairment & Revaluation
Full impairment lifecycle (indicators, assessment, recognition, reversal) plus upward/downward revaluation. Oracle and SAP offer this, but Dynamics, Workday, and NetSuite do not.

### 4. Built for the Perionyx Platform
Native integration with GL, Financial Close, Treasury, Tax, and Executive AI. No middleware required.

### 5. Modern UI
18 dark-theme components with framer-motion animations. Competitors rely on legacy or Fiori-based UIs.

### 6. Lease Accounting Readiness
ASC 842 and IFRS 16 compliance tracking built in. SAP requires separate RE-FX module. Workday and NetSuite lack lease accounting.
