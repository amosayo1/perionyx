# Approvals

## Approval Types
| Type | Description | Required For |
|---|---|---|
| Task Approval | Sign-off on completed tasks | Critical tasks, final close |
| Reconciliation | Sign-off on reconciliations | All reconciliations |
| Journal | Sign-off on journal entries | All manual entries, adjustments |
| Adjustment | Sign-off on adjustments | Reconciliation adjustments |
| Reopen | Sign-off on period reopening | Period corrections |
| Close | Sign-off on period close | Period finalization |

## Approval Workflow
1. **Request** — Originator submits for approval
2. **Queue** — Approval appears in approver's queue
3. **Review** — Approver reviews the submission
4. **Decision** — Approve, reject with comments, or escalate
5. **Notification** — Originator notified of decision

## Escalation
- Level 1: Direct supervisor
- Level 2: Department head
- Level 3: Controller/CFO
- Auto-escalation based on time elapsed

## Segregation of Duties
- Same person cannot prepare and approve
- Different persons for different approval levels
- Audit trail maintained for all approvals
- Dual approval for high-value items
