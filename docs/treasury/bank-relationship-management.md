# Bank Relationship Management

## Overview

The Bank Relationship Center maintains profiles for every banking partner, tracking relationship health, products used, service levels, and risk ratings.

## Relationship Scoring

| Score Level | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | 90-100 | Strategic partner, full product suite |
| Gold | 75-89 | Preferred partner, multiple products |
| Silver | 60-74 | Standard relationship |
| Bronze | 40-59 | Limited relationship, under review |
| At Risk | <40 | Relationship deteriorating |

## Relationship Metrics

| Metric | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Total Accounts | Breadth of relationship |
| Total Balance | Depth of relationship |
| Products Used | Product penetration |
| Service Level | Service quality tier |
| Risk Rating | Bank financial health |
| Review Recency | Last review date |

## Review Cycle

- Platinum/Gold: Annual review
- Silver: Semi-annual review
- Bronze: Quarterly review
- At Risk: Monthly review
