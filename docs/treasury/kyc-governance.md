# KYC Governance

## Overview

The KYC Compliance Center manages Know Your Customer documentation for every bank account, ensuring regulatory compliance across all jurisdictions.

## Document Types

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Corporate Charter | Legal entity registration |
| Certificate of Incumbency | Current officers/directors |
| Board Resolution | Authorization to open/operate account |
| Tax Form (W-9/W-8BEN) | Tax classification |
| Business License | Operating license |
| Articles of Incorporation | Founding documents |
| Address Proof | Registered office verification |
| AML Questionnaire | Anti-money laundering assessment |
| Source of Funds Declaration | Fund origin documentation |

## KYC Statuses

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| Complete | All documents received and verified |
| Pending | Documents submitted, awaiting review |
| Expired | Documents beyond renewal date |
| In Review | Under active review by compliance |
| Missing Documents | Required documents not yet submitted |

## Beneficial Owner Tracking

Each KYC profile tracks beneficial owners with:
- Name and identification
- Ownership percentage
- ID type and number
- Nationality
- Verification status (verified/pending/expired)

## Renewal Cycle

| Risk Classification | Renewal Frequency |
|---|---|
| Low Risk | Every 3 years |
| Medium Risk | Every 2 years |
| High Risk | Annually |
| Critical Risk | Every 6 months |

## AML Review

All KYC profiles undergo AML review with status:
- **Cleared**: Passed screening
- **Pending**: Under review
- **Flagged**: Matched watchlist/sanctions, requires escalation

## Compliance Scoring

KYC completion is measured as percentage of required documents received and verified. Scores are color-coded: 100% (emerald), ≥75% (blue), ≥50% (amber), <50% (red).
