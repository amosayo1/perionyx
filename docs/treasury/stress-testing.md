# Stress Testing

## Overview

The Stress Testing module models 15 extreme scenarios across 10 categories to assess the enterprise's resilience to severe market events.

## Stress Scenarios

| Scenario | Severity | Impact | Survival |
|---|---|---|---|
| FX Shock (20% USD move) | High | -$95M | 12 months |
| Interest Shock (300bps) | Medium | -$42M | 18 months |
| Liquidity Crisis | Critical | -$250M | 3 months |
| Bank Failure | Critical | -$180M | 2 months |
| Country Sanctions | High | -$65M | 8 months |
| Commodity Spike (50%) | Medium | -$38M | 14 months |
| Revenue Decline (30%) | High | -$120M | 6 months |
| Customer Default | High | -$85M | 9 months |
| Supply Chain Disruption | Medium | -$55M | 11 months |
| Pandemic Scenario | Critical | -$310M | 2 months |

## Impact Analysis

Each scenario evaluates:
- **Total Impact**: Dollar impact on cash position
- **Impact %**: Percentage of total cash affected
- **Cash Impact**: Immediate liquidity effect
- **EBITDA Impact**: Earnings effect
- **Liquidity Impact**: Liquidity ratio effect
- **Debt Impact**: Debt covenant implications
- **Covenant Breach**: Whether debt covenants would be breached
- **Survival Months**: Months until cash exhaustion
- **Recovery Time**: Estimated recovery period

## Severity Classification

| Survival | Severity | Response |
|---|---|---|
| >12 months | Low | Monitor |
| 6-12 months | Medium | Prepare contingency |
| 3-6 months | High | Activate mitigation |
| <3 months | Critical | Emergency action |
