# Executive Risk Overview

## Purpose

The executive risk overview provides CFOs, Group Treasurers, Risk Officers, and Controllers with immediate visibility into enterprise treasury risk exposure, policy compliance, and actionable intelligence.

## Key Questions Answered

1. **What is our total risk exposure?** — Across FX, interest rates, counterparties, countries, liquidity, and concentration
2. **How much FX risk do we carry?** — Net exposure by currency, hedge coverage
3. **Are we within policy limits?** — Policy compliance status, active breaches
4. **What keeps us up at night?** — Stress test results, VaR, critical risks
5. **What should we do?** — Recommendations, alerts, executive insights

## Executive Insights

The ExecutiveRiskInsights component surfaces 10 key observations covering:
- Largest exposures by currency and counterparty
- Highest-risk countries and counterparties
- Hedge effectiveness and coverage gaps
- Policy breach hotspots
- Trend changes in risk scores
- Concentration risk warnings

## Risk Recommendations

25 deterministic recommendations across categories:
- **FX Hedging**: Increase EUR hedge, reduce USD concentration
- **Counterparties**: Diversify banking relationships
- **Debt**: Refinance floating rate exposure
- **Liquidity**: Increase liquidity reserve
- **Concentration**: Reduce single-counterparty exposure
- **Hedging**: Rebalance portfolio for optimal coverage
- **Country**: Review high-risk country exposure

## Alerts

20 risk alerts with severity classification:
- **Info**: Policy approaching limit, review due
- **Warning**: Policy breach, concentration limit approaching
- **Critical**: Exposure exceeds limit, rating downgrade
- **Emergency**: Bank failure risk, sanctions trigger

## Analytics Charts

8 analytical charts covering:
- Risk trend over 12 months
- FX distribution by currency (long/short)
- Country exposure by composite score
- Counterparty exposure by health
- Hedge coverage by instrument
- Risk score evolution
- Policy breach frequency
- VaR trend over time
