# Risk Policies

## Overview

The Risk Policy Center manages 20 treasury risk policies across 6 categories, defining thresholds, monitoring compliance, and tracking breaches.

## Policy Categories

| Category | Polices | Key Limits |
|---|---|---|
| FX | 4 | Max exposure per currency, hedge ratio min |
| Interest Rate | 3 | Floating % max, duration max, sensitivity max |
| Credit | 4 | Counterparty limit, rating minimum, concentration |
| Liquidity | 3 | Min liquidity ratio, max cash burn, min coverage |
| Concentration | 3 | Counterparty, currency, industry limits |
| Operational | 3 | Settlement risk, documentation requirements |

## Policy Structure

Each policy defines:
- **Threshold**: Maximum/minimum allowed value
- **Operator**: < > ≤ ≥ = comparison operator
- **Current Value**: Actual measured value
- **Status**: Compliant, breached, pending review
- **Owner**: Responsible person
- **Approval Required**: Required approval level for changes
- **Last Review**: Date of last policy review
- **Next Review**: Scheduled review date

## Breach Management

When a policy threshold is exceeded:
1. Breach automatically recorded with severity classification
2. Assigned to policy owner
3. Remediation plan required
4. Escalation based on severity:
   - **Info**: Notify owner
   - **Warning**: Notify Treasury team
   - **Critical**: Notify Group Treasurer
   - **Emergency**: Notify CFO and Risk Committee

## Breach Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Unacknowledged | Breach detected, not yet reviewed |
| Acknowledged | Under investigation |
| Resolved | Remediation completed |

## Review Cycle

- **FX Policies**: Quarterly review
- **Interest Rate**: Semi-annual review
- **Credit/Concentration**: Quarterly review
- **Liquidity**: Monthly review
- **Operational**: Annual review
