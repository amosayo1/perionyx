# eBAM KPIs

## Executive Metrics

| KPI | Formula | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Total Accounts | Count of all accounts | Scale of banking operations |
| Active Accounts | Count with status = active | Operational capacity |
| Dormant Accounts | Count with status = dormant | Cost reduction opportunity |
| Restricted Accounts | Count with restrictions | Compliance risk |
| Closing Accounts | Count in closure pipeline | Operational efficiency |
| Average Balance | Sum(balances) / count | Account utility |
| Average Utilization | Sum(utilization) / count | Account efficiency |
| Compliance Score | Resolved / total issues × 100 | Governance health |
| Relationship Health | Avg relationship score | Banking partnership quality |
| KYC Completion | Complete KYC / total × 100 | Regulatory readiness |
| Mandate Coverage | Active mandates / total × 100 | Authorization coverage |
| Policy Compliance | Compliant accounts / total × 100 | Policy adherence |

## Compliance Metrics

| Category | Thresholds | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Missing Documentation | >10% of accounts | Compliance review |
| Expired Mandates | Any | Immediate renewal required |
| Expired KYC | >5% of accounts | Accelerated renewal |
| Unauthorized Signatory | Any | Emergency response |
| Policy Violation | >3 accounts | Policy review |
| Dormant Risk | >90 days | Closure review |
| Country Risk | Any high-risk jurisdiction | Enhanced monitoring |
| Sanctions Review | Any | Legal escalation |

## Banking Relationship Metrics

| Metric | Strong | Needs Attention | Critical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relationship Score | >75 | 40-75 | <40 |
| Products Used | >5 | 3-5 | <3 |
| Service Level | Premium | Standard | Basic |
| Risk Rating | Low | Medium | High/Critical |
| Review Recency | <12 months | 12-18 months | >18 months |

| Coverage Ratio | >90% | 70-90% | <70% |
