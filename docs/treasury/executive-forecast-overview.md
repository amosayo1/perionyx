# Executive Forecast Overview

## Purpose

The executive forecast overview provides CFOs and Treasury teams with immediate visibility into the enterprise cash forecast, key risks, and actionable intelligence.

## Key Questions Answered

1. **What is our projected cash position?** — Ending cash, runway, confidence
2. **What could go wrong?** — Stress tests, scenarios, risk exposure
3. **How accurate are our forecasts?** — Variance analysis, confidence trends
4. **What funding do we need?** — Funding gaps, upcoming requirements
5. **What actions should we take?** — Recommendations, alerts

## Executive Insights

The `ExecutiveCashForecastInsights` component surfaces 20 key observations covering cash runway, forecast confidence, scenario risks, variance trends, funding needs, and cash burn rates.

## Recommendations

50 AI-generated recommendations (mock intelligence) across 9 categories:
- **Increase liquidity** — Build cash reserves
- **Delay CapEx** — Preserve cash during uncertainty
- **Accelerate collections** — Reduce DSO
- **Reduce idle cash** — Optimize cash deployment
- **Increase funding** — Secure additional credit lines
- **FX hedge** — Protect against currency movements
- **Improve working capital** — Optimize DSO/DPO
- **Reduce payment cycle** — Extend DPO where possible
- **Optimize investments** — Maximize returns on surplus cash

## Charts

12 analytical charts covering:
- Cash runway, confidence, burn rate trends
- Net cash flow, forecast vs actual
- Liquidity trend, funding gap
- Scenario comparison, stress impact
- Variance waterfall, working capital trend
- Free cash flow
