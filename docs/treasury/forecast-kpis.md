# Forecast KPIs

## Executive Metrics

| KPI | Formula | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Projected Cash | Sum of ending cash across periods | Future cash position |
| Cash Runway | Cash / monthly cash burn | Days until cash exhaustion |
| Forecast Accuracy | 100% - avg(abs(variance%)) | Forecast reliability |
| Liquidity Buffer | Available cash - minimum required | Cushion against shocks |
| Funding Gap | Funding required - funding available | Shortfall to address |
| Cash Burn | Monthly avg operating + investing outflows | Cash consumption rate |
| Net Cash Flow | Total inflows - total outflows | Period cash generation |
| Forecast Confidence | Weighted confidence across periods | Overall certainty |
| Variance | Avg abs variance % across categories | Forecast precision |
| Risk Exposure | Sum of high/critical risk impacts | Total risk at stake |
| Working Capital | Current assets - current liabilities | Operational liquidity |
| Free Cash Flow | Operating CF - CapEx | Discretionary cash |

## Confidence Scoring

| Range | Label | Color |
|---|---|---|
| 90-100% | High Confidence | Emerald |
| 75-89% | Good Confidence | Blue |
| 60-74% | Moderate Confidence | Amber |
| <60% | Low Confidence | Red |

## Cash Runway Thresholds

| Days | Status | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| >180 | Healthy | Standard monitoring |
| 90-180 | Watch | Review forecast assumptions |
| 30-89 | Warning | Prepare contingency plan |
| <30 | Critical | Immediate action required |

## Variance Tolerance

| Category | Acceptable Variance | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Operating | ±8% | >15% |
| Investing | ±10% | >20% |
| Financing | ±5% | >10% |
| FX | ±3% | >8% |
| Tax | ±5% | >10% |
