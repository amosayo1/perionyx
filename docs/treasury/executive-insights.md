# Executive Insights

## Overview

The Executive Insights section provides CFOs and Treasury executives with key observations drawn from across all treasury modules, highlighting areas requiring attention or presenting opportunities.

## Insight Categories

| Category | Example |
|---|---|
| Concentration | USD concentration exceeds policy limits |
| Trends | Europe liquidity decreased 6% MoM |
| Improvements | Forecast accuracy improved to 96% |
| Expirations | Three bank mandates expire this month |
| Optimization | Idle cash exceeds target by $45M |
| Working Capital | Working capital improved 3.2% this quarter |
| Risk | Counterparty credit downgrade detected |
| Compliance | KYC renewal overdue for 4 accounts |

## Insight Severity

| Severity | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | Emerald | Favorable trend or opportunity |
| Warning | Amber | Requires attention or monitoring |
| Critical | Red | Immediate action required |

## AI Recommendations

Mock recommendations provide actionable suggestions:
- Invest excess idle cash in short-term instruments
- Reallocate liquidity between entities to optimize coverage
- Increase forecast review frequency for high-variance regions
- Renew expiring bank mandates before SLA breach
- Reduce cash concentration with single counterparty

All recommendations are mock data — no AI implementation, no LLM, no external APIs.

## Alert Prioritization

Alerts are displayed by severity:
- **Critical**: Liquidity threshold reached, bank failure risk
- **High**: Dormant account detected, forecast variance exceeded
- **Medium**: Approval pending beyond SLA, compliance reminder
- **Low**: Policy approaching limit, periodic review reminder
