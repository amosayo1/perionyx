# Scenario Planning

## Overview

The Scenario Planning Center enables treasury teams to model multiple future scenarios and understand their impact on cash position, liquidity, and funding requirements.

## Scenario Types

### Business Scenarios
| Type | Description | Typical Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Expected case | Baseline |
| Optimistic | Best case | +10-20% cash |
| Pessimistic | Worst case | -15-30% cash |
| Expansion | Growth scenario | -5-15% cash (investment) |
| Recession | Economic downturn | -20-40% cash |
| Acquisition | M&A activity | -30-50% cash |

### Shock Scenarios
| Type | Description | Typical Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Capital Raise | Equity/debt issuance | +50-100% cash |
| FX Shock | Currency devaluation 10-20% | -5-15% cash |
| Interest Shock | Rate increase 200-500bps | -3-10% cash |
| Supply Chain | Major supplier disruption | -10-20% cash |
| Customer Default | Major customer non-payment | -5-25% cash |
| Pandemic | Global health crisis | -30-50% cash |
| Custom | User-defined scenario | Variable |

## Scenario Analysis

Each scenario evaluates:
- **Projected Cash**: Ending cash position under scenario
- **Liquidity Score**: 0-100 liquidity health score
- **Funding Gap**: Additional funding required
- **Cash Runway**: Days until cash exhaustion
- **Probability**: Likelihood of scenario occurring
- **Upside/Downside**: Best/worst case variance from base

## Scenario Comparison

The ScenarioComparisonChart visualizes all 10+ scenarios side-by-side, color-coded by risk level, enabling executives to understand the full range of outcomes.
