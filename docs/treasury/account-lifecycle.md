# Account Lifecycle

## Stages

```
Requested → Opening → Pending Documentation → KYC Review → Approval → Active
                                                                              ↓
                                                                        Dormant → Restricted → Closing → Closed
```

### 1. Requested
- Account request initiated by entity/business unit
- Awaiting treasury team review
- Required documents not yet submitted

### 2. Opening
- Bank application submitted
- Documentation gathering in progress
- Legal entity verification pending

### 3. Pending Documentation
- Specific documents awaited from entity
- Corporate resolutions, tax forms, beneficial owner details
- Document tracking active

### 4. KYC Review
- All documents submitted
- Bank KYC/AML review in progress
- Beneficial owner verification underway

### 5. Approval
- Internal treasury approval
- Bank account approval pending
- Signatory mandate setup

### 6. Active (Operational)
- Account fully operational
- Transactions processing
- Regular KYC reviews scheduled

### 7. Dormant
- No activity beyond threshold (90 days)
- Monthly fees still accruing
- Review for closure or reactivation

### 8. Restricted
- Limited operations due to compliance
- May accept deposits only
- Special approvals required for transactions

### 9. Closing
- Closure initiated
- Balance transferred
- Final reconciliation in progress

### 10. Closed
- Account terminated
- Final statements issued
- Records archived per policy

## Duration Guidelines

| Stage | Expected Duration | SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Requested → Opening | 1-2 business days | 2 days |
| Opening → Documentation | 3-5 business days | 5 days |
| Documentation → KYC | 5-10 business days | 10 days |
| KYC → Approval | 3-7 business days | 7 days |
| Approval → Active | 1-2 business days | 2 days |
| Active → Dormant | 90 days inactivity | N/A |
| Closing → Closed | 5-10 business days | 10 days |
