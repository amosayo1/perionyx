# CRM Guide

## Relationship to EPIP

The Strategic CRM is a subset of EPIP focused on person and organization management. EPIP tracks everything — conversations, contributions, evidence, features, roadmaps. The CRM tracks who people are and how to engage them.

## Person Types

| Type | Description | CRM Focus |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Customer | Paying customer | Support, success, expansion |
| Prospect | Potential customer | Nurture, discovery |
| Design Partner | Active product collaborator | Deep product feedback |
| Strategic Advisor | Long-term advisor | Strategic guidance |
| Subject Matter Expert | Domain expert | Validation, insights |
| Product Contributor | Anyone who contributes | Acknowledge, engage |
| Investor | Financial investor | Updates, milestones |
| Partner | Technology/integration partner | Technical collaboration |
| Industry Contact | Network contact | Relationship building |
| Team Member | Internal team | Internal knowledge sharing |

## Relationship Stages

```
Identified → Connected → Discovery → Active Engagement
                                           ↓
                              ┌────────────┼────────────┐
                              ↓            ↓            ↓
                       Design Partner  Strategic     Customer
                                        Advisor
```

## Engagement Scoring

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 | Initial contact, needs nurturing |
| 4-6 | Active engagement, regular interaction |
| 7-8 | Strong engagement, high value contributor |
| 9-10 | Strategic relationship, core advisor/partner |

## Follow-ups

Every conversation should have a follow-up date if action items exist. Track:
- Next meeting date
- Outstanding questions
- Promised deliverables
- Introduction requests
