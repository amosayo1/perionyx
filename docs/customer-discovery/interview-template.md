## Interview Guide

### Section 1: Role & Context (5 min)

**Goal:** Understand the participant's daily responsibilities and decision scope.

```
1. Can you describe your primary responsibilities in your current role?
2. What does a typical day look like for you?
3. What decisions do you make regularly that require financial data?
4. Who depends on the information you produce?
```

### Section 2: Current Workflows (10 min)

**Goal:** Map the participant's key workflows and identify friction points.

```
1. Walk me through your [month-end close / treasury monitoring / approval / reporting] process.
2. What tools or systems do you touch during this process?
3. Where do you experience the most friction or delay?
4. How long does each step typically take?
5. What information is hardest to find when you need it?
```

### Section 3: Pain Points (10 min)

**Goal:** Identify and classify specific pain points.

```
1. If you could wave a magic wand and fix one thing about your current process, what would it be?
2. What task do you dread doing every [week / month / quarter]?
3. Have you ever made a decision with incomplete information? What happened?
4. What workaround have you built because your tools don't support something?
5. How much time do you spend on manual data entry or manipulation?
```

### Section 4: Feature Exploration (10 min)

**Goal:** Validate proposed solutions and uncover unmet needs.

```
1. [Show prototype or describe feature] — How would this fit into your workflow?
2. What would need to be true for you to trust this?
3. What's missing from this approach?
4. How would you measure success for this capability?
5. Would you pay for this? What would it be worth to your organization?
```

### Section 5: Competitive Landscape (5 min)

**Goal:** Understand current alternatives and switching drivers.

```
1. What did you use before your current tool/solution?
2. Why did you switch?
3. What would make you switch again?
4. What do you wish your current tools did better?
5. Have you evaluated any alternatives recently?
```

### Section 6: Close (5 min)

```
1. Is there anything else we should know?
2. Who else in your organization should we talk to?
3. Would you be willing to participate in a follow-up?
4. Can we contact you for further feedback?
```
