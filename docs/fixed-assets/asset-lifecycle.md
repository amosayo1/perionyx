# Asset Lifecycle

## Lifecycle States
```
requested → approved → acquired → capitalized → inService →
  ├── depreciating (ongoing, monthly entries)
  ├── transferred (between departments/cost centers/ locations)
  ├── underMaintenance (preventive or corrective)
  ├── impaired (revalued down due to impairment indicators)
  ├── revalued (fair value adjustment upward or downward)
  └── disposed (sale, scrap, donation, trade-in, abandonment)
       → retired (removed from active service)
       → archived (record retention, no financial impact)
```

## Key Phases

### 1. Request & Approval
- Asset request with justification and budget reference
- Multi-level approval based on cost thresholds
- Capital expenditure budget check

### 2. Acquisition
- Purchase, lease, construction, or internal development
- Vendor management, purchase orders, invoices
- Cost accumulation (purchase price, transportation, installation)

### 3. Capitalization
- Asset placed in service
- Depreciation method and useful life determination
- Initial journal entry to record asset and contra-liability

### 4. Depreciation
- Monthly depreciation calculation based on method
- Methods: Straight-Line, Double-Declining, Sum-of-Years-Digits, Units of Production, MACRS
- Automatic journal posting to GL
- Year-to-date and life-to-date accumulated depreciation tracking

### 5. In-Service Management
- Transfers between departments, cost centers, locations, custodians
- Preventive and corrective maintenance tracking
- Insurance policy management
- Warranty tracking

### 6. Impairment
- Triggered by impairment indicators (market decline, obsolescence, physical damage)
- Recoverable amount assessment
- Impairment loss recognition with GL journal
- Reversal if conditions improve

### 7. Revaluation
- Upward or downward fair value adjustment
- Independent appraisal support
- Revaluation surplus/deficit recognition
- Ongoing depreciation based on revalued amount

### 8. Disposal & Retirement
- Sale, scrap, donation, trade-in, or abandonment
- Gain/loss calculation (proceeds minus net book value)
- Removal from asset register
- Full audit trail
