# AI Integration

## Capabilities

### Replacement Recommendations
- Identify assets approaching end of useful life
- Compare maintenance costs vs replacement cost
- Recommend optimal replacement timing
- Estimate savings from replacement

### Useful Life Predictions
- Analyze historical maintenance patterns
- Predict remaining useful life based on condition
- Flag assets likely to need early replacement
- Recommend useful life adjustments

### Maintenance Insights
- Identify maintenance patterns and anomalies
- Predict maintenance needs before failure
- Optimize preventive maintenance scheduling
- Detect abnormal maintenance cost increases

### Asset Utilization Analysis
- Track utilization rates across asset categories
- Identify underutilized assets for redeployment
- Recommend disposal of consistently underutilized assets
- Calculate ROI per asset

### Depreciation Anomalies
- Detect unusual depreciation patterns
- Flag assets where accumulated depreciation diverges from expected
- Identify assets that should be impaired based on NBV vs fair value
- Review salvage value assumptions

### CapEx Optimization
- Analyze capital expenditure patterns
- Recommend budget allocation adjustments
- Identify lease vs buy opportunities
- Predict future CapEx needs

### Executive Summaries
- Portfolio health narrative
- Key risks and opportunities
- Capital efficiency analysis
- Period-over-period comparison
