# Executive Guide

## Overview
The Fixed Assets module provides comprehensive management of enterprise capital assets throughout their lifecycle. Designed for CFOs, Controllers, and Asset Managers, it delivers real-time visibility into asset portfolio value, depreciation, maintenance, and compliance.

## Key Capabilities

### Complete Asset Lifecycle Management
From acquisition request through disposal and retirement, every stage of the asset lifecycle is tracked with full audit trail, approval workflow, and GL integration.

### Depreciation Engine
Five depreciation methods supported with automatic schedule generation, monthly journal posting, and regulatory compliance.

### Maintenance Management
Preventive and corrective maintenance tracking with cost accumulation, downtime tracking, and vendor management.

### Impairment & Revaluation
Impairment detection based on standard indicators with automated journal entries. Fair value revaluation with independent appraisal support.

### Lease Readiness
ASC 842 and IFRS 16 compliance tracking for leased assets. Right-of-use asset and lease liability recognition.

### AI-Powered Insights
Replacement recommendations, utilization analysis, maintenance optimization, and depreciation anomaly detection.

## Business Impact
- **Accurate Valuation** — Always know the current value of your asset portfolio
- **Reduced Risk** — Impairment detection, compliance tracking, insurance coverage
- **Cost Optimization** — Maintenance vs replacement analysis, utilization improvement
- **Compliance Ready** — Full audit trail, regulatory compliance, disclosure support
- **Capital Efficiency** — CapEx planning, budget variance tracking, lease vs buy analysis
