## 9. Interview Guide

### Suggested Questions for Next Interviews

#### Opening (5 minutes)
1. What's your current role and how long have you been in it?
2. What finance systems do you use daily?
3. What's the size of your finance team?

#### Pain Points (15 minutes)
4. Walk me through your most frustrating finance workflow.
5. What takes longer than it should?
6. Where do errors most often occur?
7. What workarounds have you developed?

#### Current Solutions (10 minutes)
8. What tools do you currently use for [module]?
9. What do you like most about your current setup?
10. What would you change immediately if you could?

#### AI & Automation (10 minutes)
11. How do you currently use AI or automation in finance?
12. What would you trust an AI to do? What wouldn't you trust?
13. How important is explainability for AI decisions?

#### Future State (10 minutes)
14. If you could wave a magic wand, what would your ideal finance platform look like?
15. What's the one feature that would make you switch tools?
16. How do you see AI changing finance in the next 3 years?

#### Closing (5 minutes)
17. Would you be interested in reviewing our prototype?
18. Would you be open to a follow-up interview?
19. Who else should we talk to?

### Module-Specific Questions

#### Accounts Payable
- How do you currently match invoices to POs?
- What's your approval workflow for invoices?
- How do you handle exceptions (mismatches, duplicates)?

#### Treasury
- How do you forecast cash positions?
- What's your process for managing bank relationships?
- How do you handle multi-currency exposure?

#### General Ledger
- How do you ensure GL accuracy?
- What's your month-end close process?
- How do you handle intercompany transactions?

#### Accounts Receivable
- How do you manage collections?
- What's your credit assessment process?
- How do you handle dispute resolution?

### Interview Best Practices

1. **Record consent**: Always get explicit permission to record
2. **Follow the pain**: When someone mentions pain, dig deeper
3. **Ask for examples**: "Can you show me?" is powerful
4. **Don't pitch**: This is discovery, not sales
5. **Take notes**: Even with recording, note key moments
6. **Send thank you**: Within 24 hours, with summary
7. **Extract evidence**: Within 48 hours, link to claims
