## Decision

Conduct comprehensive Enterprise Architecture Review before proceeding to Phase 26 platform expansion.

## Context

Perionyx completed 25 phases of development. Three entire platform layers (Foundation, Runtime, Persistence) were built but never adopted. In-memory stores persist across most domains. The gap between "what the code can do" and "what a user can experience" is severe.

## Alternatives

A) Continue building (expand to Phase 26 without review) — risk: compound architectural debt
B) Pause and review (Phase 25.5) — risk: delay but identify cheap fixes
C) Rewrite from scratch — risk: lose 426K LOC of working code

## Decision: Option B — Pause and Review

Evidence-based review with 10 workstreams, 30 debt items, 20 risks, 12 readiness gates.

## Consequences

- 10 workstream reports with file:line evidence
- 30 architectural debt items identified
- 20 risks cataloged with severity and mitigation
- 12 readiness gates assessed
- Architecture score: 5.5/10 (not ready for expansion)
- Product score: 4.5/10 (not ready for customers)
- Recommended: Phase 26.0 = Foundation Wiring & Persistence (12-16w)
