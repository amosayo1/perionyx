# Chart of Accounts

## Account Categories

- **Assets** (1000-1999) — Cash, AR, Inventory, Fixed Assets, Intangibles
- **Liabilities** (2000-2999) — AP, Accruals, Debt, Deferred Revenue
- **Equity** (3000-3999) — Stock, APIC, Retained Earnings, AOCI
- **Revenue** (4000-4999) — Product, Services, Subscription
- **COGS** (5000-5999) — Direct costs, Hosting, Labor
- **Operating Expenses** (6000-6999) — Salaries, Rent, Marketing, D&A
- **Other Income/Expense** (7000-7999) — Interest, FX
- **Suspense/Memo** (9000-9999) — Clearing, Off-balance sheet

## Account Types

- Balance Sheet — Permanent accounts
- Income Statement — Temporary accounts
- Statistical — Non-financial tracking
- Memo — Off-balance sheet
- Suspense — Clearing accounts

## Hierarchy

Accounts support parent-child hierarchy with unlimited depth:
- Control accounts (e.g., Accounts Receivable)
- Summary accounts (e.g., Total Revenue)
- Natural accounts (individual postable accounts)

## Multi-GAAP Support

The COA supports multiple accounting standards:
- US-GAAP
- IFRS
- UK-GAAP
- Local GAAP
