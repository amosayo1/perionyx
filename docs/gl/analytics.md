# GL Analytics & KPIs

## Financial KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Total Revenue | Budget |
| Gross Margin | Gross Profit / Revenue | > 65% |
| Operating Margin | Operating Income / Revenue | > 35% |
| EBITDA | Operating Income + D&A | Budget |
| Net Income | Revenue - Expenses | Budget |

## Liquidity KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Current Ratio | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | > 2.0 |
| Quick Ratio | (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities | > 1.5 |
| Working Capital | Current Assets - Current Liabilities | Positive |

## Efficiency KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Cash Conversion Cycle | DSO + DIO - DPO | < 45 days |
| Expense Ratio | Total Expenses / Revenue | < 70% |

## Leverage KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Debt Ratio | Total Liabilities / Total Assets | < 0.5 |
