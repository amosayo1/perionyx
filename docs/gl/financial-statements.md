# Financial Statements

## Balance Sheet

Reports assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time.

Key metrics:
- Total Assets = Current Assets + Non-Current Assets
- Total Liabilities = Current Liabilities + Non-Current Liabilities
- Total Equity = Stock + Retained Earnings + AOCI
- Working Capital = Current Assets - Current Liabilities

## Income Statement

Reports revenue and expenses over a period.

Key metrics:
- Gross Profit = Revenue - COGS
- Operating Income = Gross Profit - Operating Expenses
- EBITDA = Operating Income + D&A
- Net Income = Total Revenue - Total Expenses

## Cash Flow Statement

Reports cash inflows and outflows by activity.

Key metrics:
- Operating Cash Flow
- Investing Cash Flow
- Financing Cash Flow
- Free Cash Flow = Operating CF - Capital Expenditures

## Retained Earnings Statement

Shows changes in retained earnings over a period.
