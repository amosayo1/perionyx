# Period Close

## Close States

1. **Open** — Period is active for posting
2. **Soft Close** — Period is closing but adjustments allowed
3. **Hard Close** — Period is finalized
4. **Locked** — Period is permanently locked
5. **Reopened** — Period was reopened for corrections

## Close Checklist

1. Journal Review — Verify all journals are posted
2. Account Reconciliation — Reconcile subledgers
3. FX Revaluation — Revalue foreign currency balances
4. Accrual Review — Review and post accruals
5. Deferred Revenue — Recognize deferred revenue
6. Intercompany Reconciliation — Balance IC accounts
7. Allocation Execution — Run cost allocations
8. Trial Balance Review — Review and approve TB
9. Management Review — Management sign-off
10. Final Close — Execute final close

## Late Journals

Late journals require special approval and reopen the period temporarily.
