# 12 — AI

## 1. The AI Doctrine

**AI explains; it never decides** (PP-101, PP-102, AI Behaviour Guide). A Perionyx AI action prepares evidence, exposes confidence, requests confirmation, and never fabricates. The human is the financial authority, always (PP-102, Ramp AI Trust Contract).

The research synthesis:
- **Stripe** proves Decision Intelligence: every recommendation carries claim, sources, reasoning, confidence, alternatives (Stripe S14.4; DI).
- **Linear** proves AI in the loop: drafting, summarizing, routing — always confirmable, never autonomous (PP-103).
- **Ramp** proves the AI Trust Contract: the eight clauses — Explain, Show evidence, Expose confidence, Request confirmation, Never fabricate, Never hide reasoning, Never bypass governance, Never replace financial authority (Ramp S13).
- **Coupa** proves AI in spend: classification and coding assistance with human review (PP-102).

## 2. The Eight Clauses (Ramp AI Trust Contract, adopted)

1. **Explain** — every AI output explains its own reasoning in plain language (PP-104).
2. **Show evidence** — every claim cites its sources, with citations opening the source (PP-105, PP-086).
3. **Expose confidence** — every recommendation carries a confidence basis (PP-106); confidence is a reasoned estimate, never a fake scalar (PP-109).
4. **Request confirmation** — AI never executes a financial action autonomously; it requests and waits (PP-107, PP-160).
5. **Never fabricate** — AI never invents numbers, sources, or citations (PP-108, DI invariant).
6. **Never hide reasoning** — reasoning is always visible, expandable, and storable (PP-110).
7. **Never bypass governance** — AI flows through permission, approval, SoD, and audit like any other action (PP-111, PP-162).
8. **Never replace financial authority** — the human signs, approves, and is responsible (PP-102).

## 3. Evidence Packages

AI actions carry the **Evidence Package** (06, DI section): claim, sources, citations, reasoning, confidence, alternatives, basis, integrity. Rendering order and components are canonical (PP-104–PP-110).

**Confidence** is measured and categorical: `Approve / Reject / Needs-review` (PP-109, PP-119). Per-capability confidence thresholds replace a universal 70% (Phase 27.1R finding): each AI action type defines its own threshold and its own needs-review trigger.

## 4. Decision Categories

Every AI recommendation resolves to a **decision category** (PP-109):

- **Approve** — above threshold, unambiguous evidence.
- **Reject** — conflicts or policy breach, with reason.
- **Needs-review** — below threshold or conflicting evidence; routes to a human.

The category renders with its confidence basis and its evidence (PP-106, PP-105). A category is never presented without the evidence that produced it.

## 5. AI Surface Contracts

- **AI context panel** — prepares evidence for a human decision, with source links (PP-101).
- **AI assistant** — conversational with citations; every claim links its source (PP-105).
- **AI classification** — suggests codes/categories with alternatives and confidence (PP-102).
- **AI summarization** — summarizes with timestamps and ranges; the original is one click away (PP-103).
- **AI forecast** — renders as a band with horizon, evidence, and scenario basis (PP-046).

Every AI surface passes the audit gate: the reasoning, inputs, model, and outputs are recorded (PP-110, PP-165).

## 6. Governance

AI is governed like finance (PP-111):

- **Permissions** — AI actions require explicit grants (PP-162); no AI bypasses IAM.
- **Rate limits** — AI endpoints are rate-limited (proxy + platform).
- **Tenant isolation** — models never see cross-tenant data; no cross-tenant learning, ever (PP-021, PP-022, constitutional).
- **Observability** — provider health, model, latency, cost, and failure tracked (AI Platform).
- **Human override** — a human can always override, reject, or correct an AI output, and the override is audited (PP-112).

## 7. AI Rules (Condensed)

1. AI explains; it never decides (PP-101, PP-102).
2. Every output carries the Evidence Package (PP-104–PP-110).
3. The eight trust clauses are absolute (Ramp S13 adopted).
4. Confidence is measured and categorical, not a fake scalar (PP-109).
5. AI requests confirmation before any financial action (PP-107).
6. AI never fabricates and never hides reasoning (PP-108, PP-110).
7. AI never bypasses governance or financial authority (PP-111, PP-102).
8. AI flows through permission, approval, SoD, and audit (PP-111, PP-162).
9. Tenants are isolated; no cross-tenant learning (PP-021).
10. A human can always override, and the override is audited (PP-112).

## 8. AI Anti-Patterns

- **The oracle** — AI output presented without evidence or confidence (rejected: PP-106).
- **The silent autopilot** — AI executing financial actions without confirmation (rejected: PP-107, PP-160).
- **The fake confidence** — a percentage that doesn't correspond to any model (rejected: PP-109).
- **The ungrounded citation** — a source the AI invented (rejected: PP-108).
- **The permissionless model** — AI routes that bypass IAM (rejected: PP-111, Phase 18.1B).
- **The hidden reasoning** — a decision the user cannot interrogate (rejected: PP-110, DI invariants).
