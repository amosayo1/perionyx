# Executive Guide

## Overview
The Accounts Receivable module provides comprehensive management of customer receivables across the entire order-to-cash lifecycle. Designed for CFOs, Treasurers, and Controllers, it delivers real-time visibility into outstanding receivables, collection efficiency, credit exposure, and cash inflow forecasts.

## Key Capabilities

### Real-Time Visibility
- Total outstanding and overdue amounts with aging breakdown
- DSO and CEI tracking with trend analysis
- Credit exposure and risk concentration monitoring
- Cash inflow forecasts for 30/60/90-day liquidity planning

### Collections Optimization
- Priority-based collection queue with collector assignment
- Automated escalation workflows
- Promise-to-pay tracking and follow-up
- Performance metrics for collection effectiveness

### Risk Management
- Credit limit monitoring with utilization alerts
- Automated risk scoring and rating
- Credit review scheduling and tracking
- High-risk customer identification

### Cash Application
- Automated receipt matching to invoices
- Unapplied receipt tracking
- Matching rate monitoring
- Exception handling workflow

### AI-Powered Insights
- Collection recommendations with prioritization
- Late payment predictions
- DSO forecasting
- Cash inflow scenario analysis
- Executive insights with natural language explanations

## Business Impact
- **Reduced DSO** — Target reduction from 45 to 35 days
- **Improved CEI** — Target improvement from 78% to 85%
- **Lower Bad Debt** — Early warning reduces write-offs
- **Better Cash Visibility** — 30/60/90-day cash inflow forecasts
- **Operational Efficiency** — Automated matching and collections
