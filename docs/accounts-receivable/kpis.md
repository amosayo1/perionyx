# AR KPIs

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Formula | Target | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) | (Avg Receivables / Revenue) × Days | < 35 days | Average time to collect payment |
| CEI (Collection Efficiency Index) | (Collections / (Beginning + New - Ending)) × 100 | > 85% | Efficiency of collection efforts |
| Overdue % | (Overdue Receivables / Total Receivables) × 100 | < 15% | Percentage of receivables past due |
| Credit Utilization | (Credit Used / Credit Limit) × 100 | < 70% | How much credit is being used |
| Collection Rate | (Collections / (Outstanding + Collections)) × 100 | > 80% | Effectiveness of collection |
| Cash Inflow 30d | Projected receipts next 30 days | Target-based | Short-term cash visibility |
| High Risk Exposure | Total exposed to high/critical risk | < $300K | Concentration risk |

## Category Groups
- **Aging**: Total Outstanding, Overdue %, Aging by Bucket
- **Efficiency**: DSO, CEI, Collection Rate
- **Credit**: Credit Utilization, High Risk Exposure
- **Cash**: Cash Inflow projections
- **Customer**: Count, Average DSO per customer
