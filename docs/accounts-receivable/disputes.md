# Dispute Management

## Dispute Reasons
- Duplicate invoice
- Incorrect amount
- Missing purchase order
- Service not rendered
- Damaged goods
- Late delivery
- Contractual disagreement
- Credit not applied
- Other

## Resolution Workflow
1. **Open** — Dispute filed by customer or system
2. **Investigating** — Assigned to adjuster, evidence gathered
3. **Resolution** — Amount agreed, credit note issued, or dispute rejected
4. **Applied** — Adjustments posted to customer account
5. **Closed** — Case completed

## Metrics
- Average resolution time
- Dispute-to-invoice ratio
- Resolution rate by reason
- Customer satisfaction score
