# AR Reporting

## Standard Reports

### Aging Report
- Customer-level aging by bucket (current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91+)
- Total overdue and overdue percentage
- Configurable as-of date

### DSO Report (Days Sales Outstanding)
- Period DSO calculation
- Best possible DSO (using only current receivables)
- Comparative period DSO
- Trend analysis

### CEI Report (Collection Efficiency Index)
- CEI calculation with beginning/ending receivables
- Collections efficiency tracking
- Target CEI comparison

### Customer Balance Report
- Current balance, credit limit, available credit
- Aging summary per customer
- Payment history summary

### Cash Forecast
- 30/60/90-day cash inflow projections
- By customer, by aging bucket
- Confidence intervals

### Outstanding Invoices
- All open invoices by customer
- Aging bucket, days overdue
- Collector assignment

### Credit Exposure
- Total credit extended and utilized
- High-risk concentration
- Limit review schedule

### Collection Performance
- Collector activity and results
- Promise-to-pay success rate
- Escalation effectiveness
