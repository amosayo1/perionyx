# AI Integration

## Capabilities

### Collection Recommendations
- Prioritized collection actions by overdue amount and days
- Suggested contact method (email, phone, escalation)
- Optimal collection timing based on payment patterns

### Credit Risk Warnings
- Early warning when credit utilization exceeds thresholds
- Risk score changes and trend analysis
- Recommended credit limit adjustments

### Late Payment Prediction
- Probability of payment within 7/15/30/60 days
- Expected payment date by invoice
- Factors contributing to delay (seasonal, customer-specific, macro)

### DSO Prediction
- Forward-looking DSO estimate based on current aging
- Scenario analysis (optimistic, base, pessimistic)
- Impact of collection actions on DSO

### Customer Payment Behavior
- Average days to pay by customer
- Payment method preferences
- Seasonal payment patterns

### Cash Inflow Forecast
- 30/60/90-day cash inflow projections
- Confidence intervals
- Scenario-based forecasting

### Executive Insights
- Natural language insights for executive dashboard
- Anomaly detection and root cause analysis
- Trend explanations and recommendations
