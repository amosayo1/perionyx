# Collections

## Workflow
1. **Identification** — System identifies overdue invoices by aging bucket
2. **Assignment** — Collector assigned based on priority and workload
3. **Activity** — Email, phone, letter, escalation actions logged
4. **Promise to Pay** — Customer commits to payment date and amount
5. **Escalation** — Level-based escalation (Level 1-3, Final)
6. **Resolution** — Payment received, dispute filed, or write-off

## Priority Matrix
| Days Overdue | Amount < $10K | $10K-$100K | > $100K |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-30 | Low | Medium | High |
| 31-60 | Medium | High | Urgent |
| 61-90 | High | Urgent | Urgent |
| 91+ | Urgent | Urgent | Urgent |

## Collector Workspace
- Queue view grouped by priority
- Customer history and contact log
- One-click email/call actions
- Promise-to-pay recording
- Escalation workflow
